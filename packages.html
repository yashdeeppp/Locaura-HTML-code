FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What is included in the shoot?▾
All packages include on-location shoot execution, script writing, creative direction, editing, captions, and hashtags. Higher-tier packages include multiple shoot days and dedicated content management.
Do I need to be on camera?▾
Not necessarily. We create content around your brand — whether that's you, your products, your space, or your team. We'll plan what works best during the strategy session.
How long does it take to start?▾
We typically kick off within 5–7 days of onboarding. The first step is a discovery call where we understand your brand, goals, and audience — then we build your content plan.
Can I upgrade my package later?▾
Absolutely. Many clients start with the Growth Starter and move up as their brand scales. We make transitions seamless with no gaps in content production.
Which platforms do you post on?▾
We primarily focus on Instagram and YouTube Shorts, but content can be adapted for Facebook, LinkedIn, and other platforms depending on your audience and goals.
Do you offer a custom plan if nothing fits?▾
Yes — the Custom Brand Package is fully bespoke. Tell us what you need and we'll build a proposal around your exact goals, timeline, and budget.
