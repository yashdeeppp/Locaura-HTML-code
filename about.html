How We Work

Our Process

From your first message to your first viral reel — here's what working with Locaura looks like.

1

Discovery Call

We learn about your business, your goals, your audience, and what's worked (or hasn't) so far. No templates — just a real conversation.

2

Strategy & Planning

We build your content strategy — pillars, hooks, posting cadence, platform focus — and share a monthly roadmap for your approval.

3

Script & Creative Direction

We write scripts, develop concepts, and plan every shoot so that when the camera rolls, everything is intentional and on-brand.

4

Shoot & Production

Our team handles all on-location production — so you show up, we handle the rest. Professional, efficient, and designed to get the best out of you.

5

Editing & Delivery

Every reel is edited with trend-aware pacing, captions, sound design, and brand-consistent style — delivered ready to post.

6

Review & Growth

We track performance, share insights, and continuously refine the strategy so every month performs better than the last.
